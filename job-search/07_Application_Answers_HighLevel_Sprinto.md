### ✍️ "Describe a problem statement you contributed to solving…"
> **Problem:** When we expanded into new international markets, onboarding hotels and DMCs was slow and inconsistent. Each partner went through a different process, compliance checks varied, and leadership had no single view of supplier performance. That delayed launches and squeezed margins.
>
> **My role:** I owned the vendor-onboarding lifecycle. I wrote SOPs and SLAs for identifying, vetting and onboarding hotels and DMCs, and added a quality-assurance framework for new partners. Using historical sales volumes, I prioritised the top-selling inventory so high-demand partners went live first. I then built a CRM + Looker Studio reporting stack so leadership could track partner KPIs in real time, without manual spreadsheets.
>
> **Impact:** Onboarding turnaround dropped significantly and new destinations launched faster. Inventory coverage grew 40%, and with the renegotiated partner terms, accommodation margins rose 7.8%. The documented process also made partner compliance and reporting consistent across regions, which is the kind of process instrumentation this role covers.

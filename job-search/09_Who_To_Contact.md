# Who to Contact: Verified Routes Only (checked 14 Nov 2026)

## Why there's no "BCC everyone" list

I didn't build a list of personal emails for HR, managers, VPs and founders, for three reasons:

1. **They aren't public.** Getting them means guessing a pattern (e.g. firstname@company.com) or buying data-broker lookups. A guessed address is a made-up contact. It may bounce, or reach the wrong person.
2. **BCC blasts backfire.** One email sent to 10+ people at the same company usually lands in spam. Recruiters who notice see it as untargeted, and it can get your address flagged.
3. **Most of these companies ask candidates to apply through their portal**, and that's where your application actually gets logged.

What works better is **one portal application per role**, plus **one or two personalised LinkedIn notes** to the recruiter or hiring manager.

## How to find the right person on LinkedIn (5 minutes per company)

1. Open the company's LinkedIn page and go to **People**.
2. Search for:
   - **Recruiter:** "talent acquisition" or "recruiter", filtered to India.
   - **Hiring manager:** the team in the job title, e.g. "supply", "procurement", "vendor", "customer success", "operations".
3. Pick **one recruiter and one hiring manager** at the manager or senior-manager level. Skip VPs and founders at large companies. At small startups (fewer than 50 people), the founder *is* the hiring manager.
4. Send a connection note of 200 characters or less. Ready-made notes are in `04_Recruiter_Outreach_and_Followups.md`, for example:
   > Hi [Name], I've applied for [Role]. I handle vendor onboarding, SLAs & supplier negotiation at 30 Sundays (+40% supply, +7.8% margin). Would love to connect. – Aditya
5. If they accept, send the LinkedIn follow-up message from the same file.
